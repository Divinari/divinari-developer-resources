# CEFR & ACTFL Proficiency Metadata Specification

Public curriculum units (`schemas/curriculum-unit.schema.json`) organize target `concept_keys` alongside transparent **CEFR** (`A1`–`C2`) and **ACTFL** proficiency descriptors plus verifiable **Can-Do** statements.

Listing alignment descriptors describes educational pedagogy and does not claim official endorsement by the Council of Europe or ACTFL.
